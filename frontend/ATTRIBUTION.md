# Product icons

The SVG files in this folder come from [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org), the
open-source emoji and icon project, and are licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

They are renamed after the product codes seeded by the migrations under
`backend/internal/infrastructure/migrations/`.

Re-download them with:

```bash
./scripts/fetch-product-icons.sh
```
